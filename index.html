Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vizura Studio LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in relation to the Freak mobile application.

At Freak, we believe in privacy by design. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, email address, or physical location.

To provide a premium gaming experience, we partner with industry-standard services:

  • PostHog: Provides anonymous analytics to help us improve game performance.

3. Data Security

Any technical data processed by our partners is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell user data to third parties.
