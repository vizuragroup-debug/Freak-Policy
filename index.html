02

AI Processing

To generate unique game prompts, the App transmits transient player nicknames and selected game modes to our secure cloud infrastructure. This data is processed by third-party AI language models in real-time.

Strict Retention Policy: This data is processed in-memory only and is never stored, logged, or persisted on our servers once the response is delivered to your device.

03

Multiplayer & Analytics

We utilize secure real-time synchronization services for multiplayer game states and industry-standard analytics tools to monitor app stability. All analytics data is anonymized and cannot be linked to your individual identity.

04

Third-Party Services

We partner with secure providers to handle specific app functions:

  • Advertising: Managed by Google AdMob (for non-premium users).
  • Payments: Handled entirely by Google Play / Apple Store.
  • In-App Purchases: Validated via secure transaction management services.
